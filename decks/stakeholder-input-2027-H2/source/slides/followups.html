Example: budgets due Fri Apr 30

Heads-up Apr 23, reminder Apr 28, due-date note Apr 30. If it is still missing, the sponsor is copied on May 4 and the escalation contact on May 7.

Each message is posted once, on your GitHub issue (and by email if enabled). A valid file stops all reminders immediately.

Reminders are automatic and posted once each. You will hear from the system a week before your due date, two days before, and on the day. Escalation to your executive sponsor only starts two business days after the due date, and to the escalation contact after five, because a late input puts the optimization run on Tue May 25 at risk.
