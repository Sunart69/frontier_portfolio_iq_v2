What this means for you

The solver can only fund what is in the workbook. Late or incomplete inputs are the main reason an initiative is deferred. Ring-fenced teams stay with their owners unless the owner approves a transfer, and every approval you are asked for shows the value it would unlock.

The inputs you send are the only facts the optimizer sees. It applies each LOB's budget, ring-fenced teams and mandatory commitments as hard rules, then finds the highest-value plan. It returns a committed portfolio we can execute, a conditional one that depends on approvals, and an unconstrained comparison.
