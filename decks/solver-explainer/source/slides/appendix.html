The model in one slide

Decisions

x[i,s]: start initiative i in month s (yes or no)

y[p,i,c,t]: hours team p gives initiative i for skill c in month t

A[p,t]: hours team p has in month t

Objective

Maximize time-adjusted value minus transfer and contractor costs

Time-indexed MILP, PuLP with the HiGHS solver, 1% optimality gap

Main constraints

Hours used ≤ A[p,t] for every team and month

Each month’s demand met exactly

Cumulative spend ≤ budget, per LOB and funding source

Eligibility, transfer limits, deadlines, dependencies

Code, tests and sample outputs: github.com/Sunart69/frontier-portfolio-iq. A full run takes about 2.5 minutes.

For analysts and engineers. The model is a time-indexed mixed-integer linear program. Binary variables choose each initiative's start month; continuous variables route team hours, directly or through transfer and release rules, with substitution and transfer efficiencies applied. Every hour is drawn from exactly one team's monthly availability. A full run solves three portfolios, twelve scenarios, a per-team release scan and the LOB reconciliation, and writes the results workbook, dashboard and executive deck.
