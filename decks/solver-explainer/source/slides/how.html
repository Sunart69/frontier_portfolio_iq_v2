Walk left to right. The workbook holds the facts each LOB confirms. The rules are the guardrails, applied as hard constraints. The search finds the best plan that obeys them. It returns three portfolios, each clearly labeled. Only the committed one is executable today; the other two exist to put a price on decisions LT has not yet made. Judgment stays with LT.
