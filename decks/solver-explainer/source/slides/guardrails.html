Eight rules the solver will never break

Ring-fenced teams work only for their owner, unless the owner authorizes

Unapproved transfers never enter the committed plan

No hour is counted twice, whatever route it takes

No LOB budget is overspent in any month

Restricted money pays only for the work it is meant for

Mandatory and critical work is never silently dropped

A shared benefit is counted once, split across LOBs

Fairness rules apply only when leadership turns them on

These are hard constraints in the model, not preferences. A plan that breaks one is never shown as feasible. If a mandatory or critical commitment cannot be met, the solver does not quietly drop it: it reports which commitment fails and why, so the conflict reaches leadership. Fairness rules such as minimum investment per LOB are available, but switched off unless leadership asks for them.
