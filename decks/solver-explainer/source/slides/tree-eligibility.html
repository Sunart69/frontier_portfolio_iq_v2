The solver runs this check for every team, initiative and month before it chooses anything. A route the tree rejects never exists in the model, so ownership is respected by construction. Step 1 covers the team's start and end months, whether its skill matches (or an allowed substitute at lower efficiency), and funding restrictions. Shared teams and the owner's own work go straight into the committed plan. Another LOB can use ring-fenced hours only if the owner authorized that initiative or approved a transfer. A pending transfer appears only in the conditional plan, priced. A rejected transfer is used only in the unconstrained comparison.
