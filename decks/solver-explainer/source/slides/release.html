Added net value versus the committed plan, one team released at a time. Each release needs the owning LOB’s approval, so this value is conditional, never committed. Ring-fencing is not wasteful in itself: it may protect regulatory, expertise or service commitments.

This is how the solver makes the cost of each restriction visible, without implying that ring-fencing is wrong. Each bar releases 30% of one team to other LOBs and re-optimizes. The value comes from other LOBs' unmet demand, not the size of the team. Wealth's AI team gives the most hours, all to Commercial. The temporary contractor cohort adds nothing because no other LOB used its released hours. These are inputs to a conversation with each owning LOB, not decisions.
